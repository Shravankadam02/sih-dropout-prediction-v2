Students should be encouraged to speak with their assigned mentor directly when: they feel persistently overwhelmed or unable to cope, they are considering dropping out or taking a long break, they are dealing with a personal or family crisis affecting their studies, they feel isolated or unsupported, or they have tried self-directed strategies without improvement over several weeks.

A mentor can offer things an AI assistant cannot: personalized institutional knowledge, direct advocacy with faculty or administration, emotional support grounded in an ongoing relationship, and the ability to take real action like adjusting deadlines or arranging support services.

Reaching out early, before a situation becomes a crisis, consistently leads to better outcomes than waiting.
