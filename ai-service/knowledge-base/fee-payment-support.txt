Fee payment difficulties are a common source of stress and can lead to students disengaging from academics even when the financial issue is temporary. Most institutions offer installment plans, fee deferral options, or scholarship/waiver programs that students often don't know about.

If fees are overdue, the most effective step is speaking with a mentor or the accounts office early — before the overdue period grows — since most flexible arrangements are easier to arrange proactively than after a large balance accumulates. Waiting and avoiding the conversation typically makes the situation harder to resolve.
