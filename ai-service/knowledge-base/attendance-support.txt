Students with low attendance often face a combination of practical and emotional barriers: transportation issues, family responsibilities, health problems, or loss of motivation after falling behind. The most effective first step is identifying the specific cause rather than assuming it's simply lack of interest.

Practical tips for improving attendance: set a fixed daily routine, identify the single hardest day of the week and plan around it specifically, talk to a mentor before missing three consecutive days rather than after, and don't let one missed week become a reason to avoid returning — the longer the gap, the harder it feels to walk back in.

If attendance is dropping due to family or financial pressure, this is exactly the kind of situation a mentor can help navigate — institutions often have flexible arrangements, fee deferrals, or counseling support that students aren't aware exists.
