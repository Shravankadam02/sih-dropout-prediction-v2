Effective study habits that improve academic performance: active recall (testing yourself instead of re-reading notes), spaced repetition (reviewing material across several days instead of once), teaching concepts to someone else to confirm real understanding, and studying in short focused blocks (45-50 minutes) with real breaks in between.

Common ineffective habits to avoid: highlighting text without engaging with it, cramming the night before an exam, and studying in an environment full of distractions like a phone within reach.

A student whose test scores are dropping despite putting in study time may benefit from changing their study method rather than simply studying more — quality and technique matter more than raw hours in most cases.
