Exam stress is common and manageable with the right approach. Effective strategies include: breaking revision into small daily blocks instead of cramming, practicing past papers under timed conditions to reduce surprise on the actual day, and prioritizing sleep over extra late-night study — research consistently shows sleep-deprived studying has poor retention.

If a student feels persistently overwhelmed, anxious, or unable to concentrate despite trying these strategies, that is a sign to talk to a mentor or counselor rather than push through alone. Academic pressure combined with unaddressed stress is one of the leading factors in disengagement and dropout.

Small, consistent study habits — even 30 focused minutes daily — outperform irregular long sessions for most students.
